Computational · FDA Preprint · 2026
The Convergence Lifecycle in Practice
Empirical evidence from 50 FDA Warning Letters.

Takes the four-stage framework into a corpus. Across 50 FDA Warning Letters citing 160 distinct violations (2016–2025), 159 / 160 converge on firm-level attribution. The convergence is not a coding artifact — it's a structural feature of regulatory enforcement language.

Computational · NTSB Preprint · 2026
Designed Attribution
How investigative documents converge responsibility — at scale, by design.

Dependency-parsing of 71,114 NTSB accident findings to map who gets named when accidents are explained. Organizational accountability appears in only 0.42–0.58% of statements. Read alongside the FDA paper: same convergence mechanism, opposite institutional direction — FDA documents converge upward onto the firm, NTSB documents converge downward onto the pilot.

Corpus: NTSB findings · n=71,114 · 14,940 events Methods: spaCy dependency parsing · cross-method triangulation Repo: sorakim-lab/ntsb-attribution-analysis PDF · forthcoming
Read together, the two papers make a sharper point than either alone: convergence is a property of investigative language, not of the underlying events. The institutional design (regulatory enforcement vs. accident investigation) determines the direction.
