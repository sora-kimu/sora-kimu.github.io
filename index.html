Hi! 👋 I'm Sora (소라 in Korean), an independent researcher grounded in four years of pharmaceutical Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) field experience. I study Regulatory HCI — how compliance systems actively structure how practitioners interpret situations, make decisions, and assign responsibility, often producing systematic patterns such as premature convergence. My work designs interface and AI-based interventions that make these underlying structures visible, expand the space of reasoning, and resist premature closure in high-stakes environments.
