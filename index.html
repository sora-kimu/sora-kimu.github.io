Hi! 👋 I'm Sora (소라 in Korean), an independent researcher developing Accountability-Aware AI — a research program investigating both how accountability is structurally produced in regulated work and how AI integration reshapes it, at the intersection of HCI, STS, and organizational technology adoption. Drawing on four years of embedded practice in pharmaceutical Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) quality control, I study how regulated organizations structure responsibility before failures occur, and what design choices support or erode those structures as AI enters regulated workflows. My work spans 14+ papers and 9 production tools used by a ~50-person quality team at a GMP-regulated manufacturing site.
