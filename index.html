Hi! 👋 I'm Sora (소라 in Korean), an independent researcher developing Accountability-Aware AI — a research program at the intersection of Science & Technology Studies, Human-Computer Interaction, and organizational technology adoption. Drawing on four years of embedded practice in pharmaceutical Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) quality control, I study how regulated organizations structure responsibility before failures occur, and how AI systems reshape what can be known and attributed. My work spans 14+ papers and 8 production tools serving 50+ users at a GMP-regulated manufacturing site.

Under Review

A four-paper lifecycle theory of accountability in regulated organizations: pre-action structuring (AAC), procedural document infrastructure (CF), investigative convergence (RC), and AI-accelerated closure (PAC).

STS · Organizational Theory
Accountability Before Action: How Organizations Structure Responsibility in Advance of Outcomes
Under review · Academy of Management Review (AMR-2026-0516)
Introduces "Accountability Before Action" (AAC) as the pre-failure structuring of responsibility through procedural routines, documentation regimes, and evaluative anticipation. Draws on Garfinkel, Latour, and Star & Ruhleder.
⚖️ AAC 🔍 Infrastructure studies 📐 Organizational theory
STS · Document Infrastructure
Cognitive Friction: Procedural Documents as Accountability Infrastructure in Regulated Organizations
Under review · Organization (ORG-26-0241)
Operationalizes cognitive friction at sentence level across a 400-sentence corpus from regulated procedural documents. Reframes document friction as accountability infrastructure rather than cognitive burden.
📄 Cognitive Friction 🔍 Document infrastructure 📊 Corpus analysis
STS · Investigative Systems
Responsibility Convergence: How Investigative Systems Stabilize Attribution
Under review · Organization Science (ORSC-MS-2026-22511)
Identifies three mechanisms through which investigative systems converge on firm-level attribution: ambiguity resolution, closure pressure, and legibility bias. Empirical anchor: 159/160 FDA Warning Letters converge on firm.
⚖️ Responsibility Convergence 📄 FDA Warning Letters 🔍 Attribution
STS · AI & Technology
Premature Accountability Convergence: How Technology Accelerates Responsibility Stabilization in Organizations
Under review · Information and Organization (INFORG-D-26-00222)
Reframes AI's role from "acceleration" to "earlier institutional closure of interpretive possibility." Identifies three mechanisms: representational compression, epistemic authority amplification, and temporal contraction.
🤖 AI accountability ⏱️ Temporal closure 🏭 Sociomateriality

HCI & Design Research

Five HCI papers on judgment-preserving design in regulated work, each tied to a deployed production tool. Together they articulate a four-layer framework for accountability infrastructure design.

HCI · Framework · TOCHI
Accountability Infrastructure Design: A Four-Layer Framework for Judgment-Preserving Systems in Regulated Work
In preparation · ACM TOCHI
Umbrella paper. Introduces a four-layer framework (accessibility, structure, coordination, reasoning) and three design principles: upstream stabilization, observability over optimization, and judgment preservation.
🗂️ Four-layer framework 🧠 Judgment preservation 📐 Design principles
HCI · AI Scaffolding · CSCW 2027
Keeping Comparison Open: An RCA Scaffolder for Preserving Interpretive Alternatives in Regulated Investigations
In preparation · CSCW 2027
Introduces AI as comparison enforcer — detecting premature closure and reopening the comparison space, rather than generating candidate explanations. N=20 user study.
🤖 AI scaffolding 🔬 Root cause analysis ⚖️ Premature convergence
HCI · Document Review
Friction Before Failure: Dissociating Procedural Legibility from Procedural Endorsement
Working paper · SSRN
Dissociation finding: immediate legibility ≠ endorsement. Checklist supports immediate comprehension (5.0); TF-IDF supports conceptual orientation (5.0) — but through distinct cognitive pathways.
🔍 Document friction 🧠 Dissociation 📋 CAPA review
HCI · AI-Assisted Retrieval
From Retrieval to Reasoning: AI-Assisted SOP Access and the Shape of Interpretive Action
Working paper · SSRN
Extends SOP accessibility from retrieval problem to interpretive condition. AI layer functions to EXPLAIN, SURFACE, and KEEP interpretive alternatives rather than resolve them.
🤖 AI-assisted retrieval 📋 SOP 🧠 Interpretive action
HCI · Cross-Document Coordination
When Documents Don't Connect: The Human as Missing Infrastructure in Regulated Document Systems
Working paper · SSRN
Identifies the human as "missing infrastructure" in cross-document accountability gaps. Loopback as method. Star & Ruhleder lineage.
👁️ Invisible work 🔗 Cross-doc coordination 🏭 Infrastructure gaps

Computational Research

A computational research program operationalizing accountability across four signal layers (detection, graph structure, attribution, distribution), applied to FDA Warning Letters.

Computational · Umbrella
Responsibility as Multi-Layer Signal: A Computational Framework for FDA Warning Letters
Working paper · SSRN · Umbrella
Integrates PAC detection (F1 0.901–0.913), Accountability Graph Extraction (star topology, Firm in-degree 0.444), Attribution Prediction (LR F1=0.725), and RDM (PCA+Ridge, w_ps=0.995) into a unified framework.
📊 Computational accountability 📄 FDA Warning Letters 🕸️ Multi-layer signal
Computational · NLP
Premature Convergence Detection in Regulatory Investigative Texts
Working paper · GitHub
Detects structural pre-convergence in FDA Warning Letters — firm-level attribution stabilizes before investigative reasoning is complete. F1 0.901–0.913.
📝 Regulatory NLP ⚖️ Convergence detection 🧪 Classification
Computational · Graph
Accountability Graph Extraction from Regulatory Texts
Working paper · SSRN
Extracts accountability graphs from FDA Warning Letters. Reveals star topology centered on "Firm" (in-degree 0.444) as structural pattern.
🕸️ Graph extraction 📊 Network analysis
Computational · Classification
Responsibility Attribution Prediction: A Classification Approach to FDA Warning Letters
Working paper · SSRN
Classification approach to attribution: Individual AUC=0.940; LR F1=0.725. 30.6% of cases reassigned from Firm to System when model-corrected.
🧪 ML classification ⚖️ Attribution
Computational · Metric
Responsibility Distribution Metric: An Entropy-Centrality Approach to Accountability Structure
Working paper · SSRN
Composite metric combining entropy and centrality. PCA+Ridge produces w_ps=0.995. Cross-framework convergence with other three papers around WL2018-05.
📈 Composite metric 🕸️ Entropy + centrality
