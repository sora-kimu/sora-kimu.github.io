Teaching · 2 artifacts

Educational artifacts for regulated work.

Two open-access teaching pieces I built for analysts entering a GMP-regulated environment — bilingual, free to use, designed to teach without lecturing.

Both pieces ran with new analysts on the team I work on. Both are research prototypes as much as teaching tools — they let me test how onboarding interfaces shape practitioner reasoning before bad habits set in. Future work: study them as interventions, not just as artifacts.
📖 AI Literacy Cookbook PDF · 15 pages · 4 chapters
How to Actually Use Claude
A GMP Quality Lab AI Literacy Series.

Four short chapters on what AI tools can and can't do for regulated analysts: capabilities & limits, data security, document writing, and workflow integration. Written from inside a working QC team — every example comes from real lab situations.

Bilingual · 한 / EN Open access CC BY 4.0
📥 Read in English 한국어로 읽기
Design rationale Most AI training in regulated settings is cautionary — long lists of don'ts. This cookbook flips it: lead with what AI can actually do for an analyst's day, then layer in the constraints. Practitioners are more likely to internalize limits when they first see why the tool is worth using at all.
Artifact 02
🎮 Pixel-art Onboarding Game Web · 5 scenarios · ~15 min
DAY ONE: GMP
A retro pixel-art onboarding game for new GMP analysts.

Five first-day scenarios — gowning, material verification, SOP retrieval, deviation reporting, sign-off. Each presents a realistic moment where the right action isn't obvious. Pick a choice; see what the regulation says — and what happens if you don't.

HTML + JS Canvas Bilingual · 한 / EN Open access
▶ Play in English 한국어로 플레이
Design rationale Standard GMP onboarding is a slide deck followed by a sign-off form — a ritual that produces a paper trail without testing whether the new analyst actually understood. The game forces a decision moment at each scenario, then teaches through the consequence. A small intervention on the first-day interaction that, traditionally, asks for nothing.
