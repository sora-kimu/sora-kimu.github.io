Hi, I'm Sora! 🐚👋
(소라 means "conch shell" in Korean — yes, the 🐚 is literally my name.)

I'm an independent researcher working in pharmaceutical GMP environments in South Korea. I build Accountability-Aware AI — systems and theory at the intersection of HCI, STS, and organizational technology adoption.

The short version: I spend a lot of time thinking about why organizational outcomes feel inevitable when they're actually designed that way. It's a great conversation starter. At parties. That I go to. Occasionally.

The slightly unhinged part 🙃

I built this entire research program by myself. No lab. No advisor. No institutional affiliation. No roadmap. Just me, a question I couldn't let go of, and a slightly alarming amount of determination.

(I once spent three weeks going in circles on a theoretical framework with absolutely no one to ask. It was fine. I'm fine. We don't talk about it.)

Over the past few years, I've developed a four-stage accountability lifecycle theory (STS), built and deployed 8 production tools at a GMP manufacturing site, run N=20 user studies on four of them, computationally analyzed FDA Warning Letters, and written 14 papers across HCI, STS, and computational methods — four of which are currently under review at organization theory journals. All while working full-time.

I built this work independently because it was the only way I knew to pursue the questions seriously. Which is probably a sign I should do a PhD. Applying Fall 2027. 🎯
