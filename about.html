About

Hi! 🙌 My name is Sora — 소라 in Korean, which means a sea shell. That's why there's a 🐚 in the corner of every page on this site. (Subtle branding, I know.)

I'm a quality engineer at a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical manufacturing site in South Korea. These days my work is mostly about finding bottlenecks in the team's workflows, building tools to fix them, and teaching the team how to actually use AI in regulated work — which is where my twelve deployed tools and most of my current research come from.

Before this job, I spent two years in Boston studying English and a year and a half in Tokyo on a working holiday visa (also studying Japanese, also drinking way too much Starbucks). I work comfortably in Korean, English, and Japanese.

I like learning things on my own. During a gap year in 2021–2022, I taught myself R, Python, and the basics of deep learning — finishing the Johns Hopkins Data Science Specialization, the Johns Hopkins Genomic Data Science series, and the UC San Diego Bioinformatics specialization on Coursera, and building a PyTorch CNN for malaria blood-cell classification because apparently that's what I do for fun. These days I keep at it with AI and NLP through weekend projects — you can find what I'm currently building on the Projects page.

Outside of all this, I'm a serious coffee person. Three years as a barista, decent enough at rosetta latte art that I'll trust you with my opinion on yours, and an iced latte habit that does not negotiate with the weather. ☕
