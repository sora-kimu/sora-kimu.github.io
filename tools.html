Tools

I build tools on two tracks. Research prototypes are evaluated in user studies and published as HCI papers. Production systems operate at a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical manufacturing site as unvalidated productivity layers. For four of my HCI papers, both exist: a deployed production tool grounding the research, and a synthetic-data prototype that made the user study externally evaluable.

Part 1 — Research-Linked Systems

Four-Layer Framework · Deployed + Studied

Each tool below exists in two forms: a production system deployed at the GMP site, and a research prototype built on synthetic data and evaluated in an N=20 user study. The research prototypes let me make claims about the design principles externally evaluable without exposing proprietary content. All four are published as standalone HCI papers.

🔬 RCA Scaffolder
AI as comparison enforcer: organizes investigation into five stages that require explicit comparison before closure, with an AI layer that detects premature convergence and reopens the comparison space.
Layer 4 · Reasoning ● Ongoing
Production Deployment

Step 3: PAC Analysis. AI detects individual-blame language, surfaces alternative causal pathways (procedural, contextual, documentation), generates reopening questions.

🔗 Document Navigation Tool
Makes implicit cross-document relationships navigable. Two studies: a Traceability Explorer and a Cognitive Navigation Explorer that operationalizes loopback behavior as a structural friction signal.

Production Deployment
Deployed cross-document linking interface over the facility's quality documentation network.
Research Prototype · N=20
Traceability Explorer + Cognitive Navigation Explorer. 150-record synthetic corpus. Identified 31 missing CAPA links, 103 broken downstream links, 21 lifecycle mismatches.

Part 2 — Internal Productivity Tools

Operational Systems · No User Study

Four additional tools built for internal efficiency at the GMP site. These were developed without a research design or user study — the goal was operational utility, not external evaluation. Included here because they are part of the four-year deployment record and contribute to the embedded practitioner-researcher profile.

📊 QC Stability Dashboard
Deadline-tracking interface for stability testing programs. Tracks 25-day testing windows and coordinates schedule across multiple programs. A primary planning surface for time-sensitive QC work.
Internal productivity tool ● Ongoing
📝 Veeva Vault Change Control Assistant
Workflow support for electronic change control submissions through Veeva Vault QMS. Guides users through required fields, flags common compliance issues, reduces submission errors.
Workflow support ● Ongoing
🧪 Reagent Inventory & Expiry Tool
Automated expiry tracking and pre-expiry alerts for QC reagents. Moves reagent management from reactive deviation handling to proactive accountability — expired reagents are a common root cause for QC deviations.
Inventory system ● Ongoing
🤖 Document Auto-Review AI
Format and compliance pre-check for procedural documents. Flags formatting, numbering, and terminology issues before formal review — reduces reviewer burden, keeps final interpretation with the human reviewer.
AI pre-check ● Ongoing
