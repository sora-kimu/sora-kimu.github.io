Two kinds: design prototypes studied with practitioners, and operational tools built and deployed for the team I work with. Each card opens to a project page.

Note All visuals here are mockups. The tools themselves are real — designed, built, and deployed for daily use — but their actual interfaces sit inside a regulated manufacturing environment and can't be shown externally for confidentiality reasons. — Design prototypes · 5

Design prototypes

Five prototypes from a four-layer framework, each studied with twenty practitioners. One paper per layer; some layers have two complementary tools.

A note on the ratings The 4.5–5.0 / 5 scores these prototypes received reflect a domain effect more than a measurement claim. Participants were from the same team I work in, and pharmaceutical quality workflows have no existing analogue for the kinds of intervention these tools propose — meaning practitioners evaluated each tool against the absence of any structured support. The high ratings say less about the tools themselves than about how little design intervention this domain has received, which is, in many ways, the larger finding.

Operational tools at work

Seven tools I built and deployed for the team I work with — not formally studied, but used daily. Each targets a specific workflow bottleneck.

Operations

QC Operations Dashboard

Live view of current QC operations across the team — tests in progress, pass rates, upcoming deadlines — replacing scattered Excel tracking.

Deployed to team
Change Management

Change Control Tracker

Surfaces what the validated system hides — titles, due dates, and the change actions inside each control, with their separate deadlines and extensions.

Deployed to team
Lab Inventory

Reagent Inventory & Expiry Tool

Stock and expiry tracking with automatic email alerts — based on lead-time rules and expiry windows the team can configure.

Deployed to team
Document AI

Document Auto-Review Assistant

Trained on master templates — highlights formatting issues, missing references, and inconsistencies in draft documents. Never auto-corrects; surfaces only.

Deployed to team
Project Management

Project Scheduling Tool

Daily morning briefing for project managers — surfaces who to check on and what to ask, derived from backward-calculated checkpoints. Includes pre-written team messages.

Deployed to team
Task Coordination

Task Manager

Assigns daily tasks across the team and tracks them through completion — managers see workload, analysts see their own schedule and overdue items.

Deployed to team
Bilingual Production

Bilingual Document Translation

Korean–English translation with a managed glossary — keeps terminology consistent across the document family, so analysts don't translate the same Korean term five different ways.

Deployed to team
