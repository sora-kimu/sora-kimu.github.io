Tools

I build tools on two tracks. Research prototypes are evaluated in user studies and published as HCI papers. Production systems operate at a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical manufacturing site as unvalidated productivity layers. Together, these tools are concrete test cases for how AI can be integrated into regulated work without eroding accountability — each one grounding a paper on how to preserve human judgment while systems scale. For the four HCI papers, deployed production tools ground the research; synthetic-data prototypes make the user studies externally evaluable. The Navigation paper is grounded in two companion tools (Traceability Layer + Behavior Tracker), bringing the deployed system count to nine.

Part 1 — Research-Linked Systems

Four-Layer Framework · Deployed + Studied

The systems below exist in two forms: production deployments at the GMP site, and research prototypes built on synthetic data and evaluated in N=20 user studies. The research prototypes let me make claims about the design principles externally evaluable without exposing proprietary content. They ground four HCI papers across the four-layer framework — accessibility, structure, coordination, reasoning — that organizes accountability infrastructure in AI-integrated regulated work. The Structure layer is unusual: it comprises two companion systems (a Traceability Layer and a Behavior Tracker) that jointly ground the Navigation paper, bringing the total to five research-linked systems across four papers.

Part 2 — Internal Productivity Tools

Operational Systems · No User Study

Four additional tools built for internal efficiency at the GMP site. These were developed without a research design or user study — the goal was operational utility, not external evaluation. Included here because they are part of the four-year deployment record and contribute to the embedded practitioner-researcher profile.

📊 QC Stability Dashboard
Deadline-tracking interface for stability testing programs. Tracks 25-day testing windows and coordinates schedule across multiple programs. A primary planning surface for time-sensitive QC work.
Internal productivity tool ● Ongoing
📝 Veeva Vault Change Control Assistant
Workflow support for electronic change control submissions through Veeva Vault QMS. Guides users through required fields, flags common compliance issues, reduces submission errors.
Workflow support ● Ongoing
🧪 Reagent Inventory & Expiry Tool
Automated expiry tracking and pre-expiry alerts for QC reagents. Moves reagent management from reactive deviation handling to proactive accountability — expired reagents are a common root cause for QC deviations.
Inventory system ● Ongoing
🤖 Document Auto-Review AI
Format and compliance pre-check for procedural documents. Flags formatting, numbering, and terminology issues before formal review — reduces reviewer burden, keeps final interpretation with the human reviewer.

📖 Glossary
Terms used across this research
GMP Practice
▸ GMPGood Manufacturing Practice
FDA-enforced standards for pharmaceutical production. Covers documentation, personnel training, facility design, equipment qualification, and quality systems. Non-compliance results in Warning Letters or facility shutdown.
In this research GMP serves as the empirical site for studying how accountability structures are produced in regulated work.
▸ SOPStandard Operating Procedure
Formal documents specifying step-by-step procedures for regulated activities. Following an SOP is both operational guidance and legal protection — practitioners who deviate bear responsibility for outcomes.
In this research SOPs are studied as accountability infrastructure. The From Retrieval to Reasoning paper examines how SOP accessibility shapes interpretation at the moment of action.
▸ CAPACorrective and Preventive Action
Systematic process triggered after quality events (deviations, complaints, failures) to identify root causes and implement corrections. Must be documented, reviewed, and closed within defined timelines.
In this research CAPA workflows are the empirical context for the Review Dashboard tool and the Friction Before Failure paper — making review burden visible before escalation.
▸ Deviation
Any departure from approved procedures, specifications, or standards in regulated manufacturing. Must be formally documented, investigated, and closed. Triggers CAPA processes.
In this research Deviations are the entry point into investigative systems where responsibility gets stabilized — studied in the Responsibility Convergence paper.
▸ RCARoot Cause Analysis
Structured investigation method for identifying underlying causes of quality events. In GMP context, must demonstrate systematic reasoning — not just proximate causes but contributing factors.
In this research RCA processes are the site where premature accountability convergence occurs. The RCA Scaffolder tool and Keeping Comparison Open paper introduce AI as "comparison enforcer" — preventing closure before alternatives are exhausted.
▸ FDA Warning Letter
Official notification from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration to firms with significant regulatory violations. Public documents. Firms must respond within 15 business days with corrective actions.
In this research A corpus of N=50 letters (160 violations, 2016–2025) serves as the empirical basis for the computational research program. Four computational frameworks operationalize accountability across detection, graph, attribution, and distribution layers.
▸ QCQuality Control
Department responsible for testing, inspection, and release of pharmaceutical products. Operates within GMP framework. Roles include analysts, reviewers, and quality managers.
In this research QC is my embedded fieldsite — four years of practice as practitioner-researcher at a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical manufacturing site. All nine deployed systems serve the ~50-person QC team.
Research Concepts
▸ Accountability Infrastructuremy concept
The structural arrangements — procedural documents, investigative systems, documentation regimes — through which organizations produce responsibility attribution. Not infrastructure for accountability, but infrastructure that produces accountability.
Core claim Responsibility is produced, not assigned — and infrastructure is where that production happens.
lineage: Star & Ruhleder (infrastructure) · Garfinkel (accountability) · Orlikowski (sociomateriality)
▸ Judgment Preservationmy design principle
Design principle for AI-integrated regulated work: systems should support practitioner judgment rather than replace it. AI functions as comparison enforcer, friction surface, and interpretive scaffold — not as attribution closer or decision maker.
Contrast with Automation-focused AI design ("replace human decision-making") and explanation-focused AI design ("justify AI's decision").
operationalized in: Four-layer framework (Accountability Infrastructure Design, TOCHI target)
