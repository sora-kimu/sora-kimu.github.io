Part 1 — Research-Linked Systems

Four-Layer Framework · Deployed + Studied

The framework organizes accountability infrastructure into four layers — accessibility, structure, coordination, reasoning — each capturing a distinct point where responsibility gets produced in regulated work, and where AI integration visibly reshapes that production. The Structure layer is grounded in two companion tools: a Traceability Layer and a Behavior Tracker.

Part 2 — Internal Productivity Tools

Operational Systems · No User Study

Four tools built to prevent specific patterns of deviation the team kept running into — missed deadlines, expired reagents, formatting errors that slow review. No user studies; the goal was operational. But the shape is the same: move accountability upstream so mistakes don't become formal deviations downstream.

📊 QC Stability Dashboard
Stability tests have a 25 working-day window — miss it, that's a due-date deviation. With 40+ studies opening a month, it's easy to lose track. The dashboard surfaces every open study with its deadline so managers can see what's at risk at a glance — shifting deviation prevention upstream, before the window closes.
Internal productivity tool● Ongoing
📝 Veeva Vault Change Control Assistant
Veeva's native interface shows change control IDs but hides due dates and descriptions behind extra clicks, which makes deadlines easy to miss. This external layer pulls that context back into view.
Workflow support● Ongoing
🧪 Reagent Inventory & Expiry Tool
Expired or out-of-stock reagents are a common reason tests get delayed — which then becomes a deviation. Sends email alerts at 3 months and 1 month before expiry, with reorder thresholds tuned per reagent. Keeps the cascade from starting.
Inventory system● Ongoing
🤖 Document Auto-Review AI
Reviewers end up spending real time on the same format/terminology issues again and again. I trained an AI on approved documents so drafts can get a pre-review pass — the AI flags what to fix before it reaches a human. Clears the noise so reviewers can focus on substantive judgment.
AI pre-check● Ongoing

📖 Glossary
Terms used across this research
GMP Practice
▸GMPGood Manufacturing Practice
FDA-enforced standards for pharmaceutical production. Non-compliance results in Warning Letters or facility shutdown.
In this researchGMP serves as the empirical site for studying how accountability structures are produced in regulated work.
▸SOPStandard Operating Procedure
Formal documents specifying step-by-step procedures. Following an SOP is both operational guidance and legal protection.
In this researchSOPs are studied as accountability infrastructure — the From Retrieval to Reasoning paper examines how accessibility shapes interpretation at the moment of action.
▸CAPACorrective and Preventive Action
Systematic process triggered after quality events to identify root causes and implement corrections.
In this researchCAPA workflows are the empirical context for the Review Dashboard and Friction Before Failure.
▸Deviation
Any departure from approved procedures or standards. Must be formally documented, investigated, and closed. Triggers CAPA processes.
In this researchDeviations are the entry point into investigative systems where responsibility gets stabilized.
▸RCARoot Cause Analysis
Structured investigation method for identifying underlying causes of quality events.
In this researchRCA is the site where premature accountability convergence occurs — the RCA Scaffolder introduces AI as "comparison enforcer."
Research Concepts
▸Accountability Infrastructuremy concept
The structural arrangements through which organizations produce responsibility attribution. Not infrastructure for accountability, but infrastructure that produces accountability.
Core claimResponsibility is produced, not assigned.
lineage:Star & Ruhleder · Garfinkel · Orlikowski
▸Judgment Preservationmy design principle
AI should support practitioner judgment rather than replace it — as comparison enforcer, friction surface, and interpretive scaffold.
Contrast withAutomation-focused design ("replace decision-making") and explanation-focused design ("justify AI's decision").
